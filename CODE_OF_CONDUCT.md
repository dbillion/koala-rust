# Code of Conduct

We want koala-rust to be a friendly place to learn, build and disagree well.
This is a short code written in our own words. It follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully. Credit others for their ideas and work.
- Respect people's time, privacy and differences.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or attention, or unwelcome contact.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained pushing of a point after a decision is made.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chats) and
when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems by contacting the maintainer, [@dbillion](https://github.com/dbillion), through
GitHub. For anything sensitive, use a private channel such as a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/dbillion/koala-rust/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately and with care.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and restrict or ban people who
break this code. We try to respond fairly and to explain our decision.
